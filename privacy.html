BIC Poster is a personal automation tool that publishes the Operator's own video content to the Operator's own TikTok and Instagram accounts on a fixed daily schedule. This Policy explains the limited data BIC Poster touches to do that.

  1. 01Who operates BIC Poster

    BIC Poster is operated by a single individual (the "Operator") solely to schedule and publish the Operator's own video content to accounts the Operator owns or manages. It has no user accounts, no public sign-up, and is not made available to other individuals.

  2. 02What data is processed

    BIC Poster processes only the following:

    • OAuth access and refresh tokens issued by TikTok and Meta, used exclusively to authenticate scheduled publish requests to the TikTok Content Posting API and the Meta Graph API.
    • Video files supplied directly by the Operator, held on the hosting server for upload and moved to an archive folder once published.
    • Operational logs (timestamps, publish status, API responses) used for monitoring and troubleshooting.

    BIC Poster does not collect data from, or about, any third party, viewer, or follower of the published content. It has no web front-end, forms, cookies, or analytics trackers.

  3. 03How data is stored and protected

    Access tokens are kept in a restricted-permission environment file (chmod 600) on a private virtual server, are never committed to source control, and are never shared with any third party. Video files remain on that same private server before and after publishing.

  4. 04How data is used

    Processed data is used solely to operate the scheduled publishing described above. It is never sold, rented, or shared with advertisers, and is never used to profile or target anyone.

  5. 05Data retention

    Access tokens are retained until they expire, are revoked, or BIC Poster is decommissioned. Published video files stay in the archive folder for the Operator's own record-keeping and can be deleted at any time.

  6. 06Your controls

    Because BIC Poster only ever acts on accounts the Operator owns, control over data access sits with the standard tools each platform provides: revoking BIC Poster's access from TikTok's or Meta's app-permissions settings immediately stops all further data processing.

  7. 07Changes to this Policy

    This Policy may be updated as BIC Poster's functionality changes. The effective date above reflects the most recent revision.
